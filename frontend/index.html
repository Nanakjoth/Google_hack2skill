How the routing works

  1. 1Clinical Triage reads the report and returns a schema-constrained severity plus a recommendation. This is the only agent allowed to call a model.
  2. 2Resource Allocator filters facilities that physically have the beds, stock and a free doctor in the right specialty. Pure arithmetic.
  3. 3Routing sends the case to the nearest facility that still has headroom, and picks the least-loaded eligible doctor there.
  4. 4Doctor decision. A report alone never holds a bed. An ICU bed and medicines are committed only when a doctor chooses Patient must be seen.
  5. 5Forecast projects 30-day demand and proposes transfers that never drain a donor below its buffer.

Try this

  • →Submit a severe dengue case, then open Queues and choose Remote review. ICU count does not move — a report never costs a bed.
  • →Submit the same case again and choose Patient must be seen. Now the ICU bed is held, on a human decision.
  • →Choose Escalate to senior and watch the case move into a senior specialty queue, recording which doctor handed it up.
  • →Use the OCR-degraded sample to see whether the model still reads it. Then remove GROQ_API_KEY to watch the conservative fallback path.
  • →Open Command Center and move stock between facilities. The donor floor is enforced, so some transfers correctly refuse.
